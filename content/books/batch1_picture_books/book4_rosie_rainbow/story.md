# Rosie's Rainbow Day
## A Story About Feelings

### Page 1 (Title Page)
**Image:** Rosie with her arms outstretched under a rainbow, emotions swirling around her in colors
**Text:**
# Rosie's Rainbow Day
*A Story About Feelings*

---

### Page 2-3 (Spread 1)
**Image:** Rosie waking up grumpy in bed, a small grey cloud above her head
**Text:**
Some days, Rosie woke up feeling grey.
Not happy. Not sad.
Just... grey.
"I don't know what I feel," she mumbled.

---

### Page 4-5 (Spread 2)
**Image:** Rosie's tower of blocks falling, her face turning red with frustration, red swirls around her
**Text:**
Then her block tower crashed!
RED bubbled up inside her—
hot and prickly.
"I'M SO MAD!" Rosie stomped.

---

### Page 6-7 (Spread 3)
**Image:** Rosie looking out rainy window sadly, blue tears on cheeks, blue tones in the scene
**Text:**
Later, rain splashed against the window.
Rosie couldn't play outside.
BLUE feelings dripped down her cheeks.
"I'm so sad," she sniffled.

---

### Page 8-9 (Spread 4)
**Image:** Mama hugging Rosie on couch, soft pink and warm colors surrounding them
**Text:**
Mama scooped Rosie up.
"All feelings are okay," Mama said softly.
"Mad feelings. Sad feelings.
They all belong."

---

### Page 10-11 (Spread 5)
**Image:** Rosie taking deep breaths, green calm spreading around her, peaceful expression
**Text:**
Rosie took three deep breaths.
In... and out.
GREEN calm spread through her,
like grass soft beneath her feet.

---

### Page 12-13 (Spread 6)
**Image:** Sun coming out, Rosie looking out window at rainbow, her face lighting up with yellow joy
**Text:**
Then the sun peeked out!
A RAINBOW stretched across the sky.
YELLOW happiness burst inside Rosie
like sunshine in her heart.

---

### Page 14-15 (Spread 7)
**Image:** Rosie dancing in puddles outside, rainbow overhead, all colors swirling joyfully around her
**Text:**
Rosie danced in the puddles,
laughing and splashing.
She had felt ALL the colors today—
and that was perfectly okay.

---

### Page 16 (Final Page)
**Image:** Rosie painting a rainbow, looking at viewer with a knowing smile, colorful scene
**Text:**
Because after every storm,
there's always a rainbow.
And every feeling—even the hard ones—
is part of YOUR beautiful rainbow too.

*The End*
