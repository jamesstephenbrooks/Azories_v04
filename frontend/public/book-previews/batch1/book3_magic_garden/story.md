# The Magic Garden
*Where Wonders Grow*

### Page 2-3 (Spread 1)
**Image:** Maya in the garden with Grandma Rose, surrounded by colorful flowers, both smiling
**Text:**
Every Saturday, Maya visited 
Grandma Rose's garden.
It was the most magical place
in the whole wide world.

### Page 4-5 (Spread 2)
**Image:** Grandma Rose giving Maya a tiny seed, Maya looking at it in her palm with wonder
**Text:**
"Today," said Grandma Rose,
"you get to plant your very own seed."
Maya looked at the tiny brown speck.
"But it's so small!" she said.

---

### Page 6-7 (Spread 3)
**Image:** Maya digging a small hole in soil, Flutter the butterfly nearby watching
**Text:**
Maya dug a little hole,
dropped in the seed,
and covered it with soft, dark soil.
A beautiful butterfly came to watch.

---

### Page 8-9 (Spread 4)
**Image:** Maya watering the soil with her red watering can, looking hopeful
**Text:**
Every day, Maya watered her seed.
She sang it little songs.
She told it stories.
But nothing happened.

---

### Page 10-11 (Spread 5)
**Image:** Maya looking sad at the empty soil patch, Grandma Rose kneeling beside her comfortingly
**Text:**
"Maybe my seed is broken," Maya sighed.
Grandma Rose smiled.
"The best things take time, little one.
Keep believing."

---

### Page 12-13 (Spread 6)
**Image:** Maya discovering a tiny green sprout, gasping with joy, Flutter circling above
**Text:**
Then one morning—
a tiny green sprout!
"GRANDMA! GRANDMA!" Maya shouted.
"It's growing! It's GROWING!"

---

### Page 14 (Final Page)
**Image:** Maya standing proudly next to a tall, beautiful sunflower, bigger than her, surrounded by butterflies
**Text:**
And Maya learned the garden's secret:
With love, patience, and a little magic,
the smallest seeds become
the most wonderful things.

*The End*
