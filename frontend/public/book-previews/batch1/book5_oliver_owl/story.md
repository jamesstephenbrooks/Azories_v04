# Oliver the Curious Owl
## Questions and Discoveries

### Page 1 (Title Page)
**Image:** Oliver perched on a branch, head tilted, stars and question marks floating around him
**Text:**
# Oliver the Curious Owl
*Questions and Discoveries*

---

### Page 2-3 (Spread 1)
**Image:** Oliver in the hollow of an oak tree with Grandpa Oak, Oliver's eyes wide with curiosity
**Text:**
Oliver was a little owl
with very BIG questions.
"Why is the sky blue?" he asked.
"Why do leaves fall down?"

---

### Page 4-5 (Spread 2)
**Image:** Oliver watching a bee on a flower, head tilted, Grandpa Oak nearby smiling
**Text:**
"Why do bees buzz?
Why is honey sweet?
Why do I have feathers
instead of furry feet?"

---

### Page 6-7 (Spread 3)
**Image:** Grandpa Oak with spectacles, wisely addressing Oliver who listens intently
**Text:**
Grandpa Oak just smiled.
"The best way to find answers, little one,
is to go and look!
The world is your teacher."

---

### Page 8-9 (Spread 4)
**Image:** Oliver flying low over forest floor, discovering mushrooms, moss, a curious squirrel watching
**Text:**
So Oliver swooped down low
and discovered squidgy mushrooms,
soft green moss,
and a squirrel who asked HIM questions!

---

### Page 10-11 (Spread 5)
**Image:** Oliver in twilight forest, fireflies glowing around him, looking amazed
**Text:**
At twilight, tiny lights appeared.
"What ARE you?" Oliver gasped.
"We're fireflies," they blinked.
"We glow to say hello!"

---

### Page 12-13 (Spread 6)
**Image:** Oliver back with Grandpa Oak, excitedly sharing all he learned, full of energy
**Text:**
Oliver raced home, bursting with news.
"I learned SO much!" he hooted.
"And now I have
even MORE questions!"

---

### Page 14 (Final Page)
**Image:** Oliver and Grandpa Oak watching sunrise together from their oak tree, peaceful and wise
**Text:**
Grandpa Oak chuckled warmly.
"Questions are the seeds of wisdom.
Keep asking, little owl.
Never stop being curious."

*The End*
